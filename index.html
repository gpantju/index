我每年有若干名研究生名额， 欢迎有意从事相关领域的各年级本科生、研究生加入我的团队！

I am an Associate Professor in the School of Computer Software, College of Intelligence and Computing at Tianjin University. I earned my Ph.D. from Tianjin University in 2012 and had the opportunity to pursue a joint doctoral program at the University of Alberta under the China Scholarship Council during 2009–2010. My research centers on image synthesis, computer vision, and multimodal fusion, with a particular focus on addressing cross-disciplinary challenges in cultural heritage preservation and municipal engineering. I also serve as an Associate Editor for the Journal of Electronic Imaging and contribute as a reviewer for several leading journals and conferences including TIP、TMM、CVPR、ACM MM、CACAIE、AIC and Signal Processing.

I have several master positions available in my lab now. If you are excited about computer vision, image synthesis and machine learning, then I am interested to hearing from you.
